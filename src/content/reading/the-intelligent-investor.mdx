title: The Intelligent Investor
date: My thoughts · Feb 2026
---

Benjamin Graham’s classic completely changes the way one looks at the stock market.
Instead of seeing investing as a high-stakes gamble, it presents it as a disciplined, rational process.
The concept of "Mr. Market" is a brilliant metaphor for the emotional volatility of the masses.
Graham teaches that true investors don't try to predict the future;
they analyze the present and buy with a margin of safety.

What stood out to me is the emphasis on temperament over intellect.
Patience, emotional control, and avoiding herd mentality are more critical than having a high IQ.
The book shifted my mindset from speculation to true ownership of businesses.

It’s not about getting rich quickly, but about not losing money and growing wealth steadily.
This philosophy of value investing is timeless, prioritizing logic over market hype.

— Amarjeet Singh
