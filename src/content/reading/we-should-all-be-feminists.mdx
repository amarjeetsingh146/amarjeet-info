---
title: We Should All Be Feminists
date: My thoughts · Feb 2026
---

Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie presents feminism not as a radical ideology,
but as a fundamental necessity for a fair and just society.
Her personal anecdotes highlight how deeply ingrained gender expectations harm both men and women.
Society teaches women to shrink themselves to be less threatening,
while simultaneously boxing men into rigid, emotionally stunted roles of dominance.

The essay made me reflect on the subtle, everyday ways sexism operates,
often unquestioned and normalized in our cultures.
Adichie’s tone is inclusive and conversational, avoiding alienation while demanding change.

She successfully argues that gender equality is not just a women's issue,
but a human issue that restricts the potential of everyone involved.
It is a brilliant, accessible call to action to rethink how we raise the next generation.

— Amarjeet Singh
