---
title: Why I am an Atheist
date: My thoughts · Feb 2026
---

Bhagat Singh’s essay is a striking display of intellectual courage and clarity.
Written in the shadow of his impending execution, it strips away the comforts of blind faith.
He argues that questioning existence and religion is not an act of arrogance,
but a necessary step for human progress and genuine liberation.

What struck me most was his emphasis on reason over tradition.
He doesn't dismiss religion out of hatred, but out of a profound commitment to truth and logic.
It takes immense strength to stand by one's convictions when facing death,
without seeking the psychological crutch of an afterlife.

This piece is a powerful reminder that true morality comes from human empathy,
not from the fear of divine punishment.
It inspires a fearless pursuit of knowledge and self-reliance.

— Amarjeet Singh
