---
title: White Nights
date: My thoughts · Feb 2026
---

White Nights is a profound exploration of loneliness and the fleeting nature of human connection.
Dostoevsky captures the essence of a dreamer—someone who lives more in their own mind than in reality.
The protagonist's brief encounter with Nastenka brings a sudden, blinding light to his isolated world.

What resonates most is the vulnerability of opening up to another person,
only to face the inevitable pain when they return to their own life.
The story doesn't end with bitterness, but with a sense of gratitude for having felt something real.

It reminds us that even a single moment of true connection can be enough to sustain a soul for a lifetime.
This short story taught me that melancholy and beauty are often inseparable.
Living entirely in dreams is safe, but it is the heartbreak of reality that makes us truly human.

— Amarjeet Singh
